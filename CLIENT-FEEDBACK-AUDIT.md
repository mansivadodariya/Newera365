# Client Feedback Audit: NewEra365 Homepage & Site

**Date:** 2026-07-13
**Scope:** Cross-check of the two client feedback documents (`doc.md` homepage refinement + `doc-1.md` "missing sections") against the _actual current codebase_.
**Method:** Five parallel read-only audits of the real component and page files (not from memory), each item verified with file:line evidence and the actual on-screen content. Independent grep confirmation of the fully-missing items.

---

## TL;DR

**The site already implements roughly 80% of what the client is asking for.** Of ~35 distinct requests across both documents:

| Status                       | Count                     | Meaning                                                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ✅ **Done**                  | ~19                       | Built and matches the client intent                                                                                                 |
| 🟡 **Partial**               | ~11                       | Present but missing a field, count, or sub-item                                                                                     |
| ❌ **Missing**               | 2 sections (+5 sub-tools) | Genuinely not built, no prior decision against it                                                                                   |
| 🔵 **Deliberate divergence** | ~3                        | Built differently _on purpose_ (design canon / prior client review). Client is re-requesting. Needs a decision, not silent rebuild. |

**The three things worth reading before anything else:**

1. **Real, buildable gaps (do these):** FAQ is at 10 of the 20+ the client wants; testimonials have no country/experience field and no photos; the security band shows 4 of 6 safeguards; there is no Mobile-vs-Desktop-vs-Web **platform comparison table**; four trading tools are missing (**Currency Converter, Trading Signals, Trading Hours, Market Holidays**); 6 of the 17 partner logos render as plain text; the homepage has no **trading-volume / countries-served** trust number; the footer is missing **Trading Hours** and a **Refund policy**; there is no **mobile-app download section**.

2. **Deliberate divergences (talk before building):** The client re-requests a **trading-dashboard hero visual** (chart/phone/floating stats). This was _explicitly rejected in a prior client review_ (a code comment reads: any chart-in-a-box "reads as broker wallpaper") in favor of the current aurora + typographic hero. Same for **big-icon "why us" cards** vs. the numbers-first "Newera Edge" band, which is a `DESIGN.md` canon choice. Reversing these silently would undo prior decisions and break the design system.

3. **Accuracy / trust caveats:** (a) Partners, testimonials, and FAQs are **CMS-seeded**, so "done in code" only shows on the live site if the seed was applied to the production DB, verify on prod. (b) The client suggests specific figures (5,000+ traders, $10B volume, 99.98% execution, 10 countries). These conflict with the site's own current numbers (e.g. **180k active traders**) and **must be true before publishing**, a regulated broker cannot advertise unverified stats.

## Cross-cutting notes & risks

- **CMS-seed vs. production.** Partners, testimonials, and FAQs are CMS-driven. They only render on the live site if `seed.ts` / `seed-testimonials.ts` were applied to the **production Neon DB**. The testimonials section self-hides when empty and was found empty on prod once before. **Action: verify these are seeded on prod.**
- **Advertising accuracy (regulated broker).** The client proposes specific figures (5,000+ traders, $10B monthly volume, 99.98% execution, 10 countries, "trusted by"). A regulated FX/CFD broker must not publish unverified stats. **Every number must be true and defensible before it ships**, and must be internally consistent (the site currently says 180k traders / 99.99% uptime / 12+ years).
- **DESIGN.md violation to fix.** `TwoPathsSection.tsx:88,152` uses `motion-safe:hover:-translate-y-1.5` (card levitation). The canon is "cards never levitate on hover." [XS fix]
- **Stop-out value.** Table shows 30%; client wrote "30/50%". Confirm the correct figure. `AccountsPage.tsx`.
- **Orphaned press strip.** The "As seen in" press-logo marquee (`TrustStripDemo`, driven by `socialProofLogos`) was removed from the homepage on 2026-07-12; that CMS field is now unused. Decide: keep removed, or restore as part of the trust section (A12).
